Formula


weight = params_b * weight_bytes     BF16=2  FP8=1  AWQ-4=0.5
         wbit in the URL is bytes, not bits
kv/tok = GQA: 2·L·kv_h·d·kv_bytes
       | MLA: L·(latent+rope)·kv_bytes
       | Gemma 3: global layers × ctx + local × window
kv_worst = kv/tok × ctx × seqs × (1 − hit × 0.75)     STANDS/PAGES uses this
kv_work  = kv/tok × min(ctx, in+out) × seqs × (1 − hit × 0.75)
runtime = 2.2 vLLM / 2.4 SGLang   process reserve, not activation
per_gpu = weight/(tp×pp) + kv_worst/tp + runtime
         KV shards on TP. PP replicates KV.
usable = vram × util
PAGES  = per_gpu > usable
in, out drive TTFT, batched-tokens, kv_work — not the 57.5 fixture.
